The short version

WordCounter stores counts, not conversations. It keeps how many words, messages, attachments, emojis and keywords each member sends in each channel, month by month, plus your server's tracking settings. It reads messages to count them, then throws the text away. It never keeps message content, files or direct messages. We never sell data or use it for ads.

01Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information WordCounter ("we", "us", or "our") collects when you use the WordCounter Discord app and this website, why, who can see it, how we protect it, and what you can do about it. Botzilla and WordCounter are separate bots from the same developer. Each has its own data and its own policy.

02Information we do not collect

WordCounter does not collect or store:

  • The text of your messages. WordCounter reads each message in a tracked channel so it can count it, then discards the text. The same applies when /analyze_chat reads past messages. Only the resulting numbers are kept.
  • Attachments. Files, images and stickers are counted, never saved.
  • Direct messages. WordCounter doesn't read or count DMs.
  • Personal details. We don't ask for or store your real name, email address, home address, phone number or passwords.
  • Your Discord password. Logging in on this website goes through Discord, so we never see your password.
  • Browsing data. This website has no analytics, ads or tracking cookies, and doesn't log page visits. Only server errors are logged, with the page address and not who visited.
  • Payment details. WordCounter is free and never handles card or bank details.

03Information we collect and store

We store what WordCounter needs to keep working after a restart, and nothing for advertising or profiling:

Discord IDs
Server, channel and member IDs. These are the numbers Discord uses to tell accounts and channels apart. We use them to attach each count to the right place and person.
Counts per member, per channel and per month
The number of words, messages, attachments (files, stickers and links) and emojis, and how many times each tracked keyword was said.
Server settings
Which channels and categories are tracked or ignored, the keywords the server's admins chose to track, and which members have had their past messages analyzed.
Records that keep counts accurate
When tracking was turned on or off and when counts were reset, so past messages are never counted twice. While /analyze_chat is running, the ID of the last message read in each channel, so it can pick up where it left off after a restart. These checkpoints are deleted when the analysis finishes.
Logs
When WordCounter joins a server, we log the server's name and ID and the owner's username and ID. When it leaves, we log the server's name and ID. When something fails, the error log can record the command or page, your username and ID, and the server and channel where it happened. Log files are kept on the bot's host, used only to fix problems, and deleted automatically after 90 days.
Join and leave notices
When WordCounter joins or leaves a server, a notice is posted in a public channel of our support server showing that server's name, icon and member count, and its owner's display name (not their username). Anyone in the support server can see them. Server and owner IDs aren't shown there. They're kept only in the log files above. These notices aren't deleted automatically. Server owners can ask us to remove the ones about their server.

Planned features will store more: the Swear Jar will count swearing per member the same way other words are counted. This policy will be updated before anything new is stored.

04Processed, not stored

These features use your data once and don't save it:

  • Live counting: each new, edited or deleted message is read, counted or recounted, and its text discarded. Only the numbers in section 03 are kept.
  • Past-message analysis (/analyze_chat): past messages are read the same way, either through Discord's message search for one member or by reading channel history for the whole server.
  • Message Word Count (right-click app): the message you pick is counted and the result shown to you. Nothing is saved.

05Third-party services

WordCounter talks only to Discord: to read messages and send replies, and on this website to confirm who you are when you log in. It doesn't send your data to any other service.

Apart from these services, our host (see section 08) and the join and leave notices in section 03, we don't share data with anyone. We never sell it or use it for ads.

06Who can see your data

  • Stats: anyone in a server can view that server's leaderboards and member stats with WordCounter's commands. Stats from one server are never shown in another.
  • Server settings: members with the Manage Server permission can see and change the server's WordCounter settings with /settings.

On this website, you see your own stats in each server you share with WordCounter. Members of a server who have the Manage Server permission also see everyone's stats in that server and can change its WordCounter settings, the same things /leaderboard and /settings show in Discord.

07This website

Logging in. If you log in here with Discord, Discord tells us your user ID, username, display name and avatar. We keep those in a signed cookie called wordcounter_session in your browser so you stay logged in for up to 30 days. The cookie is not copied into our database, and logging out deletes it.

Your servers. WordCounter asks Discord for your basic profile only, not your server list. It finds the servers you share with it from its own member lists, and checks your membership and permissions with Discord's live data every time you open a page or change a setting.

Other cookies. While you log in, a cookie called wordcounter_login holds a random value for up to 10 minutes so the login can't be started by another site. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Outside requests from your browser. Pages that show avatars or server icons load those images directly from Discord (cdn.discordapp.com). Like any site you visit, they can see your IP address. Fonts and other files are served from this site.

08Data storage and security

Counts and settings live in a database file on the bot's host that only the bot can reach.

Wispbyte, a hosting company, runs WordCounter and this website and stores the database and log files. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep access limited to the bot and the developer.

09Your rights and deletion

Data is kept until it's deleted. Removing WordCounter from a server doesn't delete that server's data. You can remove some data yourself, and ask us to remove the rest:

  • Server admins: reset a member's counts, a channel's counts, or everything for the server in /settings > Data & Reset Tools, or on this website.
  • Re-analysis: Allow re-analysis clears one member's counts so their history can be rebuilt.
  • Website: log out at any time and your session cookie is deleted.
  • Everything else: anyone can ask us to delete the data tied to their Discord user ID, and server owners can ask us to delete all of a server's data, in our support server. We'll finish within 30 days of the request.

10Children's privacy

In line with Discord's Terms of Service, WordCounter is not meant for anyone under 13 (or under the minimum legal age for digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect or ask for data from children under 13.

11Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when we add features or the law requires it. When we do, we'll change the "Last revised" date at the top of this page and announce it in our support server.

12Contact us

For privacy questions or deletion requests, ask in our Discord support server. It's the only way to contact us.
